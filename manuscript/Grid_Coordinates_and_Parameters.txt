For virtual screening grid generation, receptor boxes were centered at (x = 18.6, y = 7.4, z = 23.5 Å) for Site 1 (cryptic pocket) and (x = −9.8, y = 10.3, z = 19.9 Å) for Site 2 (alternative MMP interface), using TIMP2 (PDB 1BR9, chain A, 2.1 Å resolution) as the reference structure. Each grid measured 24 × 24 × 24 Å with 0.375 Å spacing to ensure adequate coverage of local side-chain flexibility and solvent-accessible volume.
